      Windows Themepacks

      I am honored by the fact that Microsoft has devoted three Windows Themepacks to my photographic works. Following their release, I have received encouraging messages and questions from people around the world. Overwhelmed by this feedback, I thought that some of those appreciating the images may be interested in some background on locations, motifs and shooting conditions, which you can find in the articles below.

      The themepacks and the articles behind them are free, and this site is private and non-commercial. If the images have given you some pleasure and you would like to say thanks, you can buy me a coffee.
