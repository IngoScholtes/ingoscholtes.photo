      Grand Canyon National Park

      Stretching over more than 400 kilometers, the Grand Canyon is truly a marvel of nature - and one of the few that can actually be seen from space. The sheer scale and wildness of the Grand Canyon make it an unforgettable experience, which leaves most visitors in awe. I was lucky enough to spend time exploring and photographing this incredible landscape.
